# REWIND Policy: Person Eligibility & Coverage Programmes

This document governs entity qualification and eligibility within the **REWIND Evidence Atlas**.

---

## 1. Core Principle: Configured Coverage Programmes

Rather than evaluating human significance through an arbitrary numerical score (e.g. `fame_score > 80`), REWIND uses **Rules-Based Coverage Programmes**.

A person qualifies as a **REWiND Subject** (with a dedicated chronological timeline) only if they satisfy the explicit criteria of an active coverage programme.

---

## 2. Standard Coverage Programmes

### 🏛️ Programme: Heads of State & Government
- **Criteria**: Currently or formerly served as recognized national head of state, prime minister, or premier of a sovereign state or designated autonomous territory.
- **Auto-Qualification**: **YES** (upon verification of official tenure records).

### 🕊️ Programme: Senior Diplomats & Negotiators
- **Criteria**: Principal signatory, special envoy, or chief negotiator in documented bilateral or multilateral peace accords, international treaties, or UN missions.
- **Auto-Qualification**: **YES** (with primary government/UN documentation).

### 📜 Programme: Major Religious & Spiritual Leaders
- **Criteria**: Principal leader of a recognized denomination, historic movement, international spiritual council, or patriarchate.
- **Auto-Qualification**: **Conditional** (Editorial review required for borderline institutions).

### ⚖️ Programme: Major Historical & Legal Inquiries
- **Criteria**: Primary figure, witness, or central defendant in historically significant judicial inquiries, tribunals, truth commissions, or landmark legal proceedings.
- **Auto-Qualification**: **NO** (Strict human editorial sign-off required).

### 🎭 Programme: Cultural & Public Figures
- **Criteria**: Sustained international cultural significance with extensive independent archival documentation over a multi-decade span.
- **Auto-Qualification**: **NO** (Requires human review).

---

## 3. Indexed Participant vs. REWiND Subject

REWIND strictly separates:

1. **Indexed Participant**:
   - An entity mentioned or appearing within an event record (e.g. a translator, security official, spokesperson, or secondary attendee).
   - Can be linked across events without requiring a full dedicated biographical timeline.
2. **REWiND Subject**:
   - A qualified historical figure who has satisfied an active Coverage Programme and possesses an indexed chronological time machine (`/person/[slug]`).

---

## 4. Specific Edge-Case Policies

### 👥 Family, Associates & Staff
- Being the spouse, child, sibling, or assistant of a notable figure **does not qualify** someone as a REWiND Subject.
- They may be indexed as a participant if physically present at an official summit, but do not receive automated timeline generation.

### 📱 Social Media & Follower Counts
- High social media follower counts, viral incidents, or temporary internet fame do **not** establish eligibility.
- Qualification requires independent primary/secondary archival evidence over sustained periods.

### 🛡️ Minors
- Automated discovery and publication of living minors is **strictly prohibited**.
- Historical child figures (e.g. historical monarchs) require mandatory human review.

### ⚖️ Crime Victims & Accused Individuals
- Crime victims are **never** automatically converted into biographical subjects.
- Notorious criminal accusations do not automatically qualify a person unless they are central to a documented major historic trial or inquiry.

### 🕊️ Viewpoint Neutrality & Controversy
- Inclusion in REWIND is purely forensic and **viewpoint neutral**.
- Inclusion does not signify endorsement, approval, or condemnation.
- Controversial figures who meet programme criteria are indexed according to primary archival records without ideological filtering.
