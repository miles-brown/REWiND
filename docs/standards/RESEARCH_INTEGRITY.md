# REWiND Research Integrity Standard

## 1. Principles of Scientific & Historiographical Integrity

The REWiND Evidence Atlas adheres to the principles of research integrity articulated in the UK Research Integrity Office (UKRIO) Code of Practice and international historiographical norms:

1. **Traceability**: Every conclusion in REWiND must trace directly back to primary sources with precise locators (timestamps, page numbers, catalog symbols).
2. **Transparency**: Methods of entity resolution, time-zone conversion, and geospatial positioning must be publicly documented.
3. **Reproducibility**: An independent researcher inspecting REWiND's source index must be able to reproduce our conclusions without unstated assumptions.
4. **Data Provenance**: Digital assets (transcripts, images, videos) must retain cryptographic provenance and original ingest timestamps.
5. **Preservation of Limits**: Editorial notes must candidly disclose where archives are incomplete, lost, or inaccessible.

---

## 2. Research Standard for Ingestion

Before any candidate event enters the canonical Supabase database:
- Primary transcripts or broadcast footage must be inspected by a qualified human editor or an audited verification pipeline.
- Dates and times must be verified against civil calendars and astronomical solar noon data where timestamps are disputed.
- No secondary commentary may be substituted for primary archival records when the primary records exist in public repositories.
